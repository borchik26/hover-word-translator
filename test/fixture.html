Hover translator fixture

Serve over http (python3 -m http.server 8000 from the repo root) and open http://localhost:8000/test/fixture.html. Each section lists the expected behaviour.

A1 — word, form, nothing on a space

The cat runs

“runs” → translation of “run” after ~200 ms. Nothing over the spaces.

A2 — empty space and padding

Short line

Cursor right of “line” or in the grey padding → nothing.

A3 — phrases

We left early in order to catch the train, as well as the bus.

It went as well. As expected.

in order to

Any word of “in order to” / “as well as” → phrase. “as well. As” → no 3-word phrase. Linked “order” → the single word only.

A4 — forms and case

He went home. The leaves fell. This is better. Went away. WENT AWAY.

went, leaves, better, Went, WENT → translated via lemma.

A5 — apostrophes, hyphens, non-prose

don't — don’t — John's — well-known — state-of-the-art — COVID-19 — x86 — user@mail.com

Words translate; COVID-19, x86 and the email show nothing.

A6 — lang

die Katze schläft

Привет world

German paragraph → nothing. “world” → translated.

A7 — form fields

hello editable world

Never shows inside fields or contenteditable.

A8 — viewport edges, long translation

world world world

by the way, a long translation: at least, because of.

Fixed yellow words at the right/left/top edges: the overlay stays inside the viewport; at the top edge the bubble goes below the word. Cover: the plate's right edge matches the word's right edge and a long translation grows left over the previous words, never over the next one.

A9 — scrolling

Scroll this box while the overlay is shown on a word.

The world is wide and the river runs to the sea.

Every child knows the story of the old man and the sea.

The night was cold and the people went home early.

Wheel over a shown overlay (page or box) → hidden, then re-anchored where the cursor rests.

A10 — leaving the window, selection, Esc

Select this sentence by dragging across the world of words.

Overlay hides when the cursor leaves the window, while dragging, and on Esc. Selection and copy work normally.

A11 — click-through

Click this world link

Click the link while the overlay covers it → URL hash becomes #a11-clicked.

A12 — CSP / Trusted Types

Open fixture-csp.html

Styles present, no Trusted Types errors in the console.

A13 — aggressive page CSS

The world must still look normal in the overlay.

With * {color:red!important}, div {display:none!important} and html > :not(head):not(body) {display:none!important} the overlay is intact.

A14 — iframe srcdoc

Works inside the frame.

A15 — colour schemes

The light world.

The dark world.

Emulate prefers-color-scheme light/dark in DevTools; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on both pages.

A16 — broken dictionary

Build with DICT_URL=http://localhost:8000/missing.json node scripts/bundle.mjs, reinstall, hover a word → one console warning, menu shows “⚠ Словарь не загружен”, no errors on mousemove.

A17 — cross-tab toggle

Open this page in two tabs. Disable the host from tab 1’s menu → tab 2 stops showing the overlay without reload; menu label updates.

A18 — heavy page

Performance panel: pointermove handler ≤ 2 ms, no long tasks while idling the mouse.

A19 — touch

Tap this world with touch emulation on.

A tap never leaves an overlay behind.

A20 — top layer (known limitation)

The world inside a modal dialog.

The overlay is drawn under the dialog / fullscreen element — accepted MVP limitation.
